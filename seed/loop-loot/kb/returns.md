# Returns, Replacements & Damaged Items

## Damaged, broken or wrong item
If your item arrives damaged, broken or different from what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery with a clear photo of the item (and the packaging if possible). We send a free replacement — you don't need to send the damaged item back. Replacements are dispatched within 24-48 hours and usually arrive in 3-5 days.

## Missing item
If something is missing from your parcel, message us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the open package and the invoice. We will check with the warehouse and send the missing item.

## Returns for change of mind
Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 10 days of delivery. A reverse pickup is scheduled within 48 hours. Earrings and anklets cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless damaged. After 10 days of delivery returns are not accepted.

## Exchanges
Rings and bracelets can be exchanged for a different size or design within 10 days of delivery, subject to stock.

## Refunds
Refunds are reviewed by our team. Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after approval. COD orders are refunded by UPI or as store credit (customer's choice). Shipping and COD fees are non-refundable unless the item was damaged or wrong.
