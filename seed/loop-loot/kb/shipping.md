# Shipping & Delivery

## Dispatch time
Orders are packed and dispatched within 1-2 business days (Mon-Sat). Orders placed after 4 PM or on Sundays are dispatched the next business day.

## Delivery time
- Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata): 3-5 days after dispatch.
- Rest of India: 5-8 days after dispatch.
- North-East, J&K and remote pincodes: up to 10 days.

## Couriers and tracking
We ship with Delhivery, Xpressbees, Ecom Express and Shadowfax. You get a tracking link on WhatsApp and SMS as soon as the order is dispatched. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch.

## Shipping charges
Free shipping on orders above ₹499. Below ₹499 a flat ₹49 is charged. Cash on delivery (COD) has an extra ₹30 COD fee.

## Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the parcel returns to us (RTO) and COD orders are cancelled. Prepaid orders that return are refunded minus shipping.
