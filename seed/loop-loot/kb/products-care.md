# Product Care & Materials FAQ

## What are the charms made of?
Our charms are nickel-free zinc alloy with an anti-tarnish coating. Necklaces in the Moon & Star line are 925 silver-plated brass.

## Will they tarnish?
With normal use the coating lasts 6-12 months. Keep jewellery away from perfume, sanitiser, water and sweat. Store in the pouch provided.

## Do charms fit other bracelets?
Loop Loot charms use a 5mm lobster clasp and fit all Loop Loot bracelets and most standard link chains.

## Ring sizes
Stackable rings are adjustable and fit US sizes 5 to 8.

## Bracelet size
Classic Link bracelet is 18cm with a 3cm extender (fits wrists 15-20cm).

## Gift wrapping
Every order comes in a Loop Loot pouch. The Bestie Gift Box includes a handwritten note — add the message in order notes.
