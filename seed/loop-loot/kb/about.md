# About Loop Loot & Contact

Loop Loot is a homegrown accessories brand from Bengaluru, India. Support hours: Monday to Saturday, 10 AM - 7 PM IST. Our AI assistant answers 24x7; anything that needs a person is picked up during support hours.

## Order changes
You can cancel an order or change the delivery address any time before it is shipped. After dispatch, address changes are not possible; you can refuse a COD delivery instead.

## Payment issues
If money was deducted but the order failed, it is auto-refunded by the payment gateway within 5-7 business days. Share the UPI reference number and we will check.
