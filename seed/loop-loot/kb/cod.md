# Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available on orders up to ₹3,000 across most pincodes. A ₹30 COD fee applies. Please keep exact change; couriers may not carry change. You can pay by cash or UPI to the delivery partner where supported.

## COD confirmation
For orders above ₹999 we may call or WhatsApp you to confirm the order before dispatch. Unconfirmed COD orders are cancelled after 48 hours.

## Switch to prepaid
Before dispatch you can switch a COD order to prepaid via the payment link we share on WhatsApp and save the COD fee.
