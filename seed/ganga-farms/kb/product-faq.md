# Product FAQ

## Why has my honey become solid?
Raw, unprocessed honey naturally crystallises in cool weather. This is a sign of purity, not adulteration. Place the closed jar in warm water (not boiling) for 15-20 minutes to make it liquid again. Never microwave.

## How is your ghee made?
Our A2 ghee is made by the traditional bilona method: curd from Gir cow A2 milk is hand-churned into makkhan and slowly heated. 1 litre needs about 25-30 litres of milk.

## Why is the ghee grainy?
Bilona ghee is naturally grainy (danedar) and may be semi-solid in winter and liquid in summer. Both are normal.

## Storage
Ghee: keep in a cool, dry place and use a dry spoon; no refrigeration needed. Best before 9 months. Honey: never refrigerate. Mustard oil: keep away from sunlight. Pickle: always use a dry spoon.
