# Shipping & Delivery (Ganga Farms)

Orders are packed fresh and dispatched within 1-2 days from our farm in Dehradun. Delivery takes 3-6 days in metros and 5-9 days elsewhere. We ship with Xpressbees, Delhivery and India Post (for remote pincodes).

## Charges
Free shipping above ₹799; otherwise ₹60. Cash on delivery (COD) is available up to ₹2,500 with a ₹40 fee.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link on Telegram after dispatch. India Post tracking can take 48 hours to show the first scan.
