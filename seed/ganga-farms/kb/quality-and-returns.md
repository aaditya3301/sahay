# Quality Promise, Replacements & Refunds

## Damaged, leaked or spoiled products
Food items travel in glass jars. If a jar arrives broken, leaked, or the product smells or looks spoiled, message us within 48 hours (2 days) of delivery with a photo. We send a fresh replacement free of cost. No need to return the damaged item.

## Returns
For food safety reasons, perishable food products (ghee, honey, oil, jaggery, atta, pickles) cannot be returned once delivered. Non-food items like herbal tea tins can be returned unopened within 7 days.

## Refunds
Small refunds up to ₹300 are processed automatically as store credit or UPI. Larger refunds are reviewed by our team within 24 hours. Prepaid refunds reach the original payment method in 5-7 business days.

## Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once packed and shipped they cannot be cancelled because the food is packed fresh for you.
